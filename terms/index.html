What Budget is

Budget is a tool for tracking your own spending and (in Business mode) running basic invoicing and expense records for a small business. It reads till slips and bank statements you provide, and turns them into a budget you can see and act on.

Budget is not a bank, does not move money, cannot make payments on your behalf, and never asks for your online banking username or password.
